1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly (name, email address, password) when you create an account, enroll in courses, or contact us. We also collect usage data such as lessons completed and positions analyzed to power your progress dashboard.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate ChessLearn, send transactional emails (enrollment confirmations, payment receipts, email verification), display your personalized dashboard, and improve the platform. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Payments

Payment processing is handled by Stripe. We do not store your card details. Stripe's privacy policy governs the handling of your payment information.

4. Email Communications

We send transactional emails essential to your account (verification, receipts). You may also receive occasional product updates. You can unsubscribe from non-essential emails at any time.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at privacy@chesslearn.com.
